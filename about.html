Our Roots

The Story of Foodyverse

Born in Assam.
Inspired by India.

Founded by Sajal Baruah

Located in the serene landscapes of Pabhoi Panchali, S. Baruah Foodyverse was established with a singular vision: to create a dining sanctuary where nature and culinary craftsmanship coexist perfectly.

Our restaurant is more than just a place to eat; it is an open-air garden experience. With a cozy capacity of 25–28 guests, we prioritize intimacy, warmth, and unparalleled Assamese hospitality.

While our roots proudly reflect Assamese traditions through signature dishes like Masor Tenga and local Gahori Mankho, our menu takes you on a rich journey across India's diverse culinary landscape.
