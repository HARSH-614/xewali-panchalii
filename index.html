Welcome to S. Baruah Foodyverse

Where Nature Meets Culinary Craftsmanship

Founded by Sajal Baruah, S. Baruah Foodyverse is designed as an immersive sanctuary in Pabhoi Panchali. Set amidst lush greenery and open garden spaces with a cozy 25–28 guest capacity, we offer an intimate dining experience that celebrates local ingredients and culinary traditions.

Every dish is carefully prepared to evoke warmth, peace, and authenticity—bringing together the rich biodiversity of Assam and the diverse culinary legacy of India.

Roots & Environment

A Sanctuary of Greenery and Tradition

Our restaurant is built around the concept of harmony with nature. Dining at S. Baruah Foodyverse means breathing in fresh country air surrounded by trees, plants, and natural sunlight.

We honor Assamese culture not merely through our menu, but through our architecture, hospitality, and peaceful open-air garden seating designed for families, couples, and friends.
