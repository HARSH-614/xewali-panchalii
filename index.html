Rooted Here. Inspired Everywhere.

Xewali is a modern culinary journey deeply connected to the soil of Assam. Named after the fragrant night-flowering jasmine, our establishment embodies hospitality, freshness, and the quiet beauty of a peaceful evening.

We are driven by a profound respect for traditional Assamese recipes—passed down through generations—while simultaneously embracing the vast, beautiful diversity of Indian regional cuisine. We believe food should bring people together, bridging heritage with modern life.

Whether you seek the comforting tang of a classic Masor Tenga or the rich, aromatic spices of a North Indian delicacy, our kitchen prepares every dish with uncompromising authenticity and warmth.

A Taste of Assam

Experience the subtle, distinct flavours of the North-East. From the alkaline perfection of Khar to the comforting warmth of duck with ash gourd, our Assamese preparations strictly adhere to authentic methodologies and locally sourced ingredients.

  • Khar & Pitika Preparations
  • Authentic Masor Tenga
  • Pork with Bamboo Shoot

The Hands Behind the Flavour

Chef's Philosophy

"Great food is not just about complex techniques; it is about memory, respect for the ingredient, and the joy of feeding others. My journey began in an Assamese kitchen, learning the delicate balance of a perfect Tenga. Today, my mission is to present those cherished local flavours with modern elegance, while honoring the magnificent diversity of Indian regional cooking on the same table."
