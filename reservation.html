Dining Guidelines

  • Confirmation: Submitting this form sends a request to our team. Your table is not confirmed until you receive a direct message or call from us.
  • Operating Hours: We are open Tuesday to Sunday, 11:00 AM – 10:30 PM. (Closed on Mondays).
  • Grace Period: We hold reserved tables for up to 20 minutes past the booking time.
  • Large Groups: For parties larger than 10, a pre-order might be required to ensure smooth service.
  • Garden Seating: Outdoor seating is subject to weather conditions. In case of rain, we will do our best to accommodate you indoors.
